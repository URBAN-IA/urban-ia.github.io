About

University of Seoul
Urban Planning and Design

I am an integrated M.S.-Ph.D. student in Urban Planning and Design at the University of Seoul, working with Prof. Myounggu Kang in the Urban Planning and Real Estate Lab.

My work combines transport network analysis and spatial data science to examine accessibility, spatial equity and regional economic activity. I contribute to public-sector research on national transport networks and innovation cities.

서울시립대학교에서 도시공학을 공부하며, 대중교통 네트워크와 접근성, 공간 형평성, 지역 경제활동의 관계를 연구합니다.

Research interests

Public transport networks
& accessibility

Building national-scale multimodal networks from General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) data, and measuring how transport services connect places.

GTFS · Network science
Accessibility and centrality analysis

Spatial equity

Examining geographic differences in transport connectivity and access to opportunities using grid-based population and economic data.

Regional development

Investigating relationships between public transport networks and economic activity in and around national innovation clusters.

Methods & tools Python, GeoPandas, graph-tool, Dask, GIS, high-performance computing and GPU acceleration.
